Boost is publicly released three times a year in April, August, and December. Each release has updates to existing libraries, and any new libraries that have passed the rigorous acceptance process.

Read release process

Achievements

Achievements capture your contributions to Boost — automatically tracked where possible, manually verified for high-value activities.

  • 01
    Code Commits Commits authored to any Boost repository. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Code Commits.
  • 01
    Documentation Doc contributions using standardised Boost tooling (Antora / BoostLook). With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Documentation.
  • 01
    Library Authoring Authored an original Boost library accepted into the collection. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Library Authoring.
  • 01
    Library Maintenance Number of libraries actively maintained. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Library Maintenance.
  • 01
    Library Review Formal review submissions on Boost proposals. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Library Review.
  • 10
    Library Versioning Authored new versions / releases of Boost libraries. With 10 contributions, you will earn the bronze badge for Library Versioning.
  • 01
    Mailing List Participation Participation on the Boost developers mailing list. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for Mailing List Participation.
  • 01
    News Posts Published Articles / news posts published on Boost.org. With 1 contribution, you will earn the bronze badge for News Posts Published.

Badges

Badges recognize your journey on Boost — from the contributions you make to the time you've invested and the milestones you've reached along the way.

  • Achievement-based Reflects the depth of your contributions. Accumulate achievements to unlock five tiers; Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond.
  • Tenure-based Awarded in recognition of your time on the platform. The longer you've been part of the Boost community, the higher the tier you unlock.
  • Boost day celebration A celebration of the day you joined Boost. Awarded annually to mark another year as part of the community.
