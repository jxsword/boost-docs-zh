dynamic_buffer

dynamic_buffer 函数用于从 std::basic_string 或 std::vector 创建动态调整大小的缓冲区.

创建一个新的动态缓冲区来表示给定的 string.

template<
    typename Elem,
    typename Traits,
    typename Allocator>
dynamic_string_buffer< Elem, Traits, Allocator > dynamic_buffer(
    std::basic_string< Elem, Traits, Allocator > & data);
