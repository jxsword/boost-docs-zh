experimental::awaitable_operators::operator&& (6 个重载中的第 2 个)

等待两个操作都成功.

template<
    typename U,
    typename Executor>
awaitable< U, Executor > operator&&(
    awaitable< void, Executor > t,
    awaitable< U, Executor > u);

如果一个操作失败, 则取消另一个操作, 因为 AND 条件已无法满足.
