函数模板 operator!=

boost::stl_interfaces::operator!=

概要

// In header: <boost/stl_interfaces/iterator_interface.hpp>


template<typename IteratorInterface1, typename IteratorInterface2> 
  decltype(v1_dtl::derived_iterator(lhs), !(lhs==rhs)) 
  operator!=(IteratorInterface1 lhs, IteratorInterface2 rhs);

说明

operator!=() 的实现, 适用于派生自 iterator_interface 的所有迭代器.
